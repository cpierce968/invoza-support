About Invoza

Invoza is an invoicing app for managing business information, clients, jobs, estimates, invoices, price lists, invoice PDFs, and optional AI-assisted invoice drafting.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create and send an invoice?

Create or review an invoice in the app, then use the available delivery options to prepare its PDF and email. Review the recipient, amount, due date, and message before sending.

What is Create with AI?

Create with AI can prepare a reviewable invoice draft from the information you provide. Review every client detail, line item, amount, tax setting, discount, and payment term before saving or sending an invoice.

Why does Create with AI require a membership?

Managed AI invoice drafting requires an active Invoza Member subscription and a signed-in account so the app can verify access before creating a draft.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your device, open Apple’s subscription settings for your Apple Account and select Invoza. You can also use the Manage Subscription link shown in Invoza when your membership is active.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Invoza Member screen and select Restore Purchases. Invoza asks StoreKit to synchronize eligible Apple purchases, then refreshes your membership status.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to More → Account → Delete Account and confirm the action. The app explains that this permanently erases local invoices, clients, jobs, estimates, and settings; for businesses you own, associated cloud reminder state is removed. If deletion is in progress or temporarily unavailable, wait and try again later.

Can I import a client from my contacts?

Yes. When you choose Import from Contacts, Invoza requests access to the selected contact information to prefill the client form. You can instead enter client details manually.
