What Invoza provides

Invoza provides tools for managing business information, clients, jobs, invoices, estimates, price lists, invoice PDFs, and optional invoice reminders. Basic invoicing functionality is available without an Invoza Member subscription. Managed AI invoice drafting requires an active membership and an authenticated account.

Do not treat an AI draft as accounting, tax, legal, or professional advice.

You can use Restore Purchases in Invoza to ask StoreKit to synchronize eligible purchases. You can manage or cancel a subscription through Apple’s subscription settings. Invoza does not promise refunds; Apple’s applicable policies govern purchase and refund questions.
