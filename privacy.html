Privacy Policy

This policy describes information handled by the Invoza app and the services it uses to provide its features.

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Information Invoza handles

Account and authentication information
When you sign in, Invoza uses Apple or Google sign-in through Supabase authentication. The account identity and available profile details, such as a name or email address, may be used to maintain your session and associate your account with a business.
Business, client, invoice, estimate, job, and price-list information
Invoza lets you create and manage business information, client contact details, invoices, estimates, jobs, pricing, payment terms, and related settings. The app stores its working business records on your device. Some information needed for connected features, such as account membership and invoice-reminder state, is handled by Invoza’s backend services.
Optional Contacts access
If you choose to import a client from your device’s Contacts picker, Invoza reads the selected contact details to prefill the client form. You can continue entering a client manually if you do not grant Contacts access.
Subscriptions
Invoza uses Apple’s StoreKit to offer and restore memberships. Apple manages the purchase transaction. For managed AI access, Invoza sends a verified transaction representation to its backend so the membership can be checked.
AI-assisted invoice requests
If you use Create with AI, the invoice request text and related request context needed to prepare the draft are sent to Invoza’s backend. The backend sends that request to OpenAI to produce a structured, review-only invoice draft. You review the draft before saving it; Invoza does not automatically create or send an invoice from the AI response.
Email delivery and reminders
When you choose to send an invoice with your device’s mail tools, your email provider handles the message. If automatic invoice reminders are enabled and available, Invoza processes the information needed to prepare and send the reminder, including relevant business and client email details, invoice number, due date, and outstanding amount.

How information is used

  • To provide invoicing, estimates, client, job, and business-management features.
  • To authenticate you, maintain account and business access, and respond to account actions.
  • To check an active membership before allowing managed AI invoice drafting.
  • To provide an AI draft when you request it and to let you review it before using it.
  • To schedule, send, or record invoice reminders when that optional feature is enabled and available.

Service providers

Invoza uses service providers to operate features. Based on the current app and backend implementation, these include:

  • Apple for Sign in with Apple, StoreKit purchases, and Apple-provided device services.
  • Google when you choose Google sign-in.
  • Supabase for authentication and related backend account services.
  • OpenAI for AI-assisted invoice drafting when you submit a Create with AI request.
  • Email delivery providers used for available automatic reminder delivery.

These providers handle information as needed to provide their respective services. Their own privacy terms may also apply.

Choices and controls

  • You choose whether to grant Contacts access and can manage that permission in your device settings.
  • You choose whether to use Create with AI and review the resulting draft before saving it.
  • You can manage automatic invoice reminders in Invoza where the feature is available.
  • You can manage Apple subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings and use Restore Purchases in Invoza.
  • You can request account deletion from the Account screen in Invoza.

Account deletion

The Account screen includes a Delete Account action. The app states that deletion permanently erases invoices, clients, jobs, estimates, and settings stored on the device. For businesses you own, associated cloud reminder state is removed; other members may lose access to those businesses. If deletion cannot be completed immediately, Invoza may show that deletion is in progress or temporarily unavailable.

Security and storage

Invoza uses device storage for local business records and uses connected services for the features described above. No specific retention period, encryption representation, or security guarantee is made in this policy. Avoid entering information you do not want processed by the services required for a feature.
